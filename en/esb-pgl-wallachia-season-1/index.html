PGL Wallachia 2024

ClientESB
Production TypeEsports broadcast / commentary set design
Overview

Art direction and set design for the broadcast coverage of PGL Wallachia 2024, produced by ESB for the Latin American audience.

The project focused on creating a thematic environment for commentators aligned with the visual identity of the tournament.

Concept & Art Direction
  • Research of the tournament branding and visual direction
  • Development of a medieval-inspired scenic concept
  • Integration of the tournament’s visual language into the set design
Set Design
  • Scenic backings and architectural elements inspired by medieval aesthetics
  • Custom central desk design for commentators
  • Selection of furniture, props and decorative elements
Production & Implementation
  • Art department budget management
  • Procurement of furniture and scenic props
  • Production coordination and set installation
  • Final set dressing and styling
OutcomeA thematic broadcast set aligned with the tournament’s identity, providing a visually cohesive environment for live commentary and analysis.
