Evidencia Oculta 2025

Evidencia Oculta

ClientAmérica Televisión / 14y6
Production TypeInvestigative television series with dramatized reconstructions of real criminal cases.
Overview

Art direction for Evidencia Oculta, an investigative television series focused on the reconstruction of real homicide cases in Peru.

The project required adapting existing locations and designing temporary sets to support the dramatization of each case while maintaining visual coherence and production efficiency across multiple episodes.

Art Direction
  • Design and adaptation of sets for crime reenactments
  • Set dressing and environmental storytelling for each case
  • Selection and management of props to build narrative spaces
  • Costume coordination for actors involved in dramatizations
Art Department Production
  • Management of wardrobe and prop inventory from production storage
  • Sourcing and purchasing additional props and wardrobe pieces
  • Coordination with suppliers and production departments
  • Budget management and optimization of art department resources
On-Set Supervision
  • Supervision of set preparation and installation before filming
  • Art direction presence during shooting
  • Real-time problem solving during production
  • Maintaining visual continuity across different dramatizations
OutcomeThe project delivered believable environments that allowed each case to be visually reconstructed while maintaining aesthetic consistency and practical functionality for television production.
