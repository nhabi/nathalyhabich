NathalyHabich

Nathaly Habich is an interior architect and art director. She designs sets, studios and spaces made for the camera: television shows, streaming studios, esports broadcasts and brand experiences for events.

For her, color is not a surface layer but matter: it shapes, transforms and tells stories. That approach, together with more than ten years designing spaces and a diploma in Art Direction for Film and Audiovisual Media from UNAM, defines the studio’s work.

Thestudio

Nathaly Habich Studio is an art direction and production design studio based in Lima, working on projects throughout Peru. The studio designs, produces and supervises every space until it is ready to shoot, with an art direction, production and props team, and partners in construction and scenic finishes.

Areas of work

Production design · Sets for television and streaming · Esports broadcasts · Advertising · Brand experiences for events and activations
