Vieja Moralidad

Vieja Moralidad – Production Design Study

ClientUNAM — Art Direction Specialization
Production TypeProduction design based on screenplay
Overview

Production design exercise developed during the Art Direction specialization at UNAM, focused on creating the visual proposal for the film Vieja Moralidad through screenplay analysis.

Concept & Art Direction
  • Development of the film’s visual concept
  • Research of period aesthetics and visual references
  • Definition of the project’s color narrative
Scenic Design
  • Set design based on key scenes from the screenplay
  • Environmental storytelling through props and decor
  • Definition of objects and scenic elements within each space
Visual Development
  • Costume proposals aligned with the narrative context
  • Lighting style and atmosphere definition
  • Composition and framing proposals for scenes
Technical Development
  • 3D modeling of key sets
  • Scenic floor plans
  • Digital set maquettes
OutcomeA comprehensive production design proposal integrating visual concept, scenic design and spatial planning.
